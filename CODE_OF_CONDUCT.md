# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include using
welcoming language, respecting differing viewpoints, accepting constructive
criticism, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, trolling, personal attacks, public
or private intimidation, and publishing private information without permission.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards
and may take appropriate action in response to unacceptable behavior.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces and whenever an individual
represents the project or its community.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the project maintainers. All reports will be
reviewed and investigated fairly and confidentially when possible.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers will follow a graduated response, from correction and warning to a
temporary or permanent ban, depending on the severity and circumstances.
